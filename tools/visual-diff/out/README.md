# out

## Purpose
Where a capture lands. Every file here is a photograph or a measurement of a page
at one moment on one machine — evidence for a comparison, never an input to one.

## Owns
- The capture artefacts: `<name>.json`, `<name>.png`, `<name>.rail.png`, and the
  `.collapsed` variants taken with nobody pointing at the rail.
- Composites written by `pixels.py`.

## Does NOT own
- Anything that has to survive. Nothing here is committed: the contents are
  regenerated by `capture.py` in seconds, and a stale capture that looks current
  is worse than no capture.

## Key Files
- `lw.json` / `lw.png` / `lw.rail.png`: the live LessWrong post — the reference.
- `mockup.*`: our content under their design — the control.
- `ours.*`: the ARENA notebook — the subject.
- `*.collapsed.*`: the same three with the rail closed.

## Data & External Dependencies
- Written only by `capture.py` and `pixels.py`, one directory up.

## How It Works (Flow)
1. `python3 capture.py <name>` writes `<name>.json` plus two screenshots.
2. `python3 diff.py lw ours` reads two of those JSONs.
3. `python3 pixels.py lw ours` reads two of the rail crops and writes a
   side-by-side composite back here.

## Invariants & Constraints
- 🔴 GITIGNORED, AND MEANT TO BE. `tools/visual-diff/out/*.png` and `*.json` are
  in `.gitignore`; committing a capture pins a moment of a live third-party page
  into the repo and invites someone to trust it a month later.
- 🔴 A CAPTURE IS ONLY VALID FOR THE VIEWPORT IT WAS TAKEN AT. Each JSON records
  `capturedWith` — viewport and hover point. Comparing captures taken at
  different sizes measures the window, not the design.
- The rail crops are clipped in DOCUMENT coordinates at `window.scrollY`; a crop
  taken at `y: 0` photographs the top of the page, where a fixed rail is not.

## Extension Points
- Nothing is extended here. Add capture behaviour in `capture.py`.

## Known Issues, Recurring Bugs, and Pain Points (and How to Prevent Them)

- **A capture is quietly out of date** — `ACTIVE`
  - When it happens: a stylesheet changes and only one side is re-captured.
  - Symptom: `diff.py` reports a difference that was fixed, or agreement that
    was never true.
  - Root cause: the JSONs carry no relationship to the files they measured.
  - Prevention/fix: re-capture BOTH sides before reading a diff. It is two
    commands and about ten seconds.

- **The rail is photographed closed** — `RESOLVED`
  - Symptom: an empty `*.rail.png` and `toc_label` measured at zero opacity.
  - Root cause: the synthetic hover landed before the rail existed.
  - Prevention/fix: the target's `ready` expression now waits for
    `.anb-toc-row`. Check `capturedWith` in the JSON if a rail looks wrong.
