# ARENA Curriculum

Welcome. This Book is the navigable companion to the [ARENA](https://www.arena.education/) curriculum, surfaced inside the Delta Drills practice environment.

## How to use this Book

1. **Read** the chapter or section you want to study using the left sidebar.
2. **Click "Open in Colab"** (rocket icon, top right of any notebook page) to launch the exercise in Google Colab with a free GPU.
3. **Solve the exercise** in Colab. ARENA's notebooks are self-contained — instructions, scaffolding, and tests live in the same notebook.
4. **Return to Delta Drills** to see your mastery update and pick the next thing to practice.

## What's in here

- **Chapter 0 — Fundamentals.** PyTorch, ray tracing, CNNs, optimization, backprop, VAEs/GANs. The grounding chapter.
- **Chapter 1 — Transformer Interpretability.** Mechanistic interpretability, TransformerLens, SAEs, circuits.
- **Chapter 2 — Reinforcement Learning.** Multi-armed bandits, DQN, policy gradient, PPO, RLHF.
- **Chapter 3 — LLM Evaluations.** Building evals, dataset generation, the Inspect framework, LLM agents.

## Coverage notes
